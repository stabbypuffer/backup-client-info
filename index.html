Across Project Backups

A private desktop utility for backing up its owner's selected local files to the owner's cloud storage and checking that those backups can be recovered.

Google Drive access is limited to files created or explicitly authorized for this application. The utility can upload backup files, inspect their metadata, download them for verification or recovery, and manage them under the owner's configured retention policy. It is intended for its owner's personal use, not public sign-ups.

This website describes the utility. It does not contain backup data, offer a login, or request access to your Google account.
